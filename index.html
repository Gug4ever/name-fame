The Concept

A meaningful gift.
A lasting keepsake.

One name, one book. Each volume of Name Fame is devoted to a single first name — a celebration of the name and of the person who carries it.

Inside, you discover the origins and meaning of the name, its journey through history, its symbolism and numerology, and the qualities associated with it across cultures and time.

Then you meet ten remarkable people who shared the name — leaders, creators, innovators and trailblazers who left their mark on the world. Ten stories of talent, courage and inspiration, each illustrated in delicate watercolour.

Whether for a birthday, a birth, a milestone or just because, a Name Fame book is a reminder that every name has a story worth celebrating — and that its next chapter belongs to the reader.

The Four Emblems

The Symbols of a Name

Long before surnames existed, tradition wove each given name into the fabric of the world: a planet to guide it, a stone to guard it, a flower to represent it, a colour to dress it. Every Name Fame book unveils the four emblems of its name — chosen from the great library of symbols below.

The Number Hidden in the Letters

Discover Your Name

Numerology is one of the oldest games humanity has played with the alphabet. In the Pythagorean tradition every letter carries a value from 1 to 9. Add the letters of a name, reduce the total to a single digit, and you obtain the name's number — and with it a planet, a stone, a flower and a colour. Type any first name and watch its portrait appear.
