The Concept

A meaningful gift.
A lasting keepsake.

One name, one book. Each volume of Name Fame is devoted to a single first name — a celebration of the name and of the person who carries it.

Inside, you discover the origins and meaning of the name, its journey through history, its symbolism and numerology, and the qualities associated with it across cultures and time.

Then you meet ten remarkable people who shared the name — leaders, creators, innovators and trailblazers who left their mark on the world. Ten stories of talent, courage and inspiration, each illustrated in delicate watercolour.

Whether for a birthday, a birth, a milestone or just because, a Name Fame book is a reminder that every name has a story worth celebrating — and that its next chapter belongs to the reader.

Focus

The Symbols of a Name

Long before surnames existed, tradition wove each given name into the fabric of the world: a planet to guide it, a stone to guard it, a flower to represent it, a colour to dress it. Every Name Fame book unveils the four emblems of its name — and the story behind each one.

✦ The Guiding Planet

In classical astrology every name resonates with a celestial body. A name ruled by Jupiter speaks of generosity and leadership; one ruled by the Moon of intuition and imagination; by Venus of harmony and art; by the Sun of vitality and presence. The book explains which planet watches over the name — and why.

✦ The Gemstone

From the deep blue sapphire of wisdom to the emerald of rebirth, the ruby of passion and the pearl of purity — each name is paired with a stone whose virtues mirror its character. A talisman in mineral form, chosen from centuries of lore.

✦ The Flower

The Victorian language of flowers gave every bloom a meaning: the white lily for nobility of heart, the rose for love, the thistle for resilience, the violet for modest strength. Each book presents the flower of its name and what it whispers about those who carry it.

✦ The Colour

Finally, a colour: royal blue for loyalty and depth, warm gold for radiance, emerald green for growth, violet for imagination. The shade a name wears is the shade of its temperament — and it colours the whole book.

Focus

The Number Hidden in the Letters

Numerology is one of the oldest games humanity has played with the alphabet. In the Pythagorean tradition, every letter carries a value from 1 to 9. Add the letters of a name, reduce the total to a single digit, and you obtain the name's number — a small portrait in arithmetic. Every Name Fame book computes it letter by letter, in a gold-and-navy table.
