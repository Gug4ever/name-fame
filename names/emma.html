Emma

Emma means: whole — entire unto herself.

Number 5 — The Free Spirit Mercury ✦ Diamond ✦ Rose ✦ Midnight Blue

The Story of the Name

Emma comes from the Germanic root Ermen, meaning 'whole' or 'universal': completeness in two syllables. Born as a short form of grand old names like Ermentrude, it outlived them all. Queens of England and Hawaii carried it, Jane Austen gave it to a novel, and the twenty-first century made it the world's favourite again.

The Numerology of Emma

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Emma add up to the number 5 — “The Free Spirit”. Fives are the free spirits of the number chart: movement, wit and the courage to choose their own road. A Five collects horizons the way others collect keepsakes; she charms a room, and then leaves it in search of a better question. Curious, quick and impossible to fence in, she would rather make an interesting mistake than a boring success. Life, to her, is a verb.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Diamond, the flower Rose and the colour Midnight Blue.
