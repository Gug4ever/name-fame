The Story of the Name

Francis began as a nickname. A cloth merchant of Assisi, back from trading in France, called his son Francesco, the little Frenchman, and the boy grew into the saint who made the name Europe's own. Behind it stand the Franks, the Germanic people whose name became France itself, and whose free status gave English the word frank. Its bearers have kept that openness: a saint who preached to birds, a painter who drew what nobody dared, a scientist who read the shape of life.

The Numerology of Francis

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Francis add up to the number 7 — “The Quiet Seeker”. Sevens go looking for what is underneath. They are not satisfied with the surface of a thing, and every Francis in this book took something the world thought settled and asked what it was really made of: a molecule, a mind, a monarchy, a rule of faith. A Seven works alone for a long time and then explains, plainly, what he found. The plainness is the gift.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Marigold and the colour Forest Green.
