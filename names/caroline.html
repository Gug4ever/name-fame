Caroline

The Story of the Name

Caroline is the feminine of Charles, and so it carries the same Germanic karl — free man, free woman. Latin turned it into Carolina, Italy sang it, France smoothed it to Caroline, and a German princess called Caroline of Ansbach brought it to the British throne in 1727. It has been a queen's name and a comet-hunter's name ever since, and it has never once stopped meaning free.

The Numerology of Caroline

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Caroline add up to the number 5 — “The Free Spirit”. Fives are the travellers of the number chart, and freedom is the whole of their engine. They want the next country, the next idea, the next way of doing a thing nobody has tried yet, and they are restless in the best sense: curious rather than flighty, quick to learn and quicker to adapt. Give a Five a closed door and she will find the window, and then hold it open for everyone behind her.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Lapis Lazuli, the flower Chrysanthemum and the colour Pale Blue.
