Raphael

Raphael means: God has healed.

Number 7 — The Healing Mind Neptune ✦ Amethyst ✦ Aster ✦ Deep Crimson

The Story of the Name

Raphael comes from the Hebrew Refa'el, 'God has healed': the name of the angel who walks a boy called Tobias to Media and back, cures his father's blindness with a fish, and only reveals himself at the journey's end. It has been worn since by a painter whose name became a byword for grace, by a lawyer who gave the world a word it badly needed, and by a boy from Mallorca who made a clay court his kingdom.

The Numerology of Raphael

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Raphael add up to the number 7 — “The Healing Mind”. Sevens are the deep readers of the number chart. They look longer than other people, at a canvas, a court, a page of law, until they see what is really there, and then they act with a calm that looks like ease and is in fact preparation. A Seven does not need the room's attention to know he is right; he needs time, and he uses it well.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Aster and the colour Deep Crimson.
