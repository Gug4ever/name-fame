The Story of the Name

Mary comes from the Hebrew Miryam — a name whose meaning scholars still debate: beloved, wished-for child, drop of the sea. Rome heard it as stella maris, star of the sea. It is the most borne female name in Western history — carried by the mother of Jesus, by queens, scientists, artists and nurses, and by more daughters than any other name on record.

The Numerology of Mary

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Mary add up to the number 3 — “The Creator”. Threes are the makers and the tellers of the number chart: the number of expression, imagination and joy that insists on being shared. Ideas bloom in their hands and become a story, a painting, a discovery or a song. A Three's true gift is turning what she feels inside into something the whole world can hold in its hands, and Marys have been doing exactly that for centuries.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone Deep Sapphire, the flower Poppy and the colour Slate.
