Timothy

Timothy means: one who honours God.

Number 11 — The Beacon The Moon ✦ Aquamarine ✦ Carnation ✦ Tidal Teal

The Story of the Name

Timothy is Greek arithmetic of a kind: timē, honour, added to theos, god, gives Timotheos, the one who honours God. The New Testament carried it west as Timotheus, the Tudor Bible clipped it to Timothy, and England has been shortening it to Tim ever since. Its bearers keep turning up where honour is a working habit rather than a decoration: a shorthand, a web given away, a spacewalk.

The Numerology of Timothy

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Timothy add up to the number 11 — “The Beacon”. Eleven is the master number that refuses to reduce, and it behaves like a light left on: steady, deliberate, visible a long way off. An Eleven is rarely the loudest person in the room and is usually the one the others steer by. He works at the honest, unglamorous part of a thing until it holds, then hands it over and goes back to work.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Moon, the gemstone Aquamarine, the flower Carnation and the colour Tidal Teal.
