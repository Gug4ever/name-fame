The Story of the Name

Gabriel comes from the Hebrew Gavri'el, built from gever, a strong man, and El, God: 'God is my strength', or 'man of God'. He is the angel sent to explain a vision to Daniel, to silence a doubting priest in the temple, and to stand in a house in Nazareth and wait for an answer; Muslim tradition knows him as Jibril, who brought the Qur'an word by word. Three scriptures, one errand, and a name that has barely shifted in two thousand years.

The Numerology of Gabriel

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Gabriel add up to the number 9 — “The Messenger”. Nines close the number cycle, and they behave like people carrying something that belongs to somebody else. A Nine is not interested in keeping the news; he wants it delivered, plainly, to the person who needs it. That makes him direct, sometimes uncomfortably so, and it makes him generous, because a message held back is no use to anyone.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mars, the gemstone Emerald, the flower Aster and the colour Royal Blue.
