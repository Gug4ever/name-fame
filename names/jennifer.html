The Story of the Name

Jennifer is Cornwall's own spelling of Guinevere, the Welsh Gwenhwyfar: gwen, white and fair, joined to a second half the scholars still argue over, smooth, or wave, or ghost. For centuries it hardly left Cornwall. Then a Cornish heroine walked onto Bernard Shaw's London stage in 1906, the name crossed the Atlantic, and for fifteen years running it was the one American parents chose above every other.

The Numerology of Jennifer

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Jennifer add up to the number 9 — “The Finisher”. Nines close the number cycle, and they carry themselves like people who know how a story ends: they are the ones who see the long job through. A Nine builds the whole set, medal by medal, award by award, concert by concert, and then, having finished, gives most of it away. She is warm in a practical way, which is the useful kind, and she keeps her word to the people who were there at the start.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mars, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Marigold and the colour Midnight Blue.
