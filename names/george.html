The Story of the Name

George comes from the Greek geōrgos, 'earth-worker', the farmer: the humblest of meanings for a name that slew dragons and ruled empires. A Roman soldier-martyr made it holy, a legend gave it a dragon, England took it for a patron and six of its kings, and Georges have been quietly cultivating the world ever since: nations, railways, music, ideas.

The Numerology of George

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of George add up to the number 3 — “The Creator”. Threes turn work into wonder. This is the number of expression and of fertile imagination: the mind that plants an idea in the morning and harvests a song, a machine or an entire nation by nightfall. It is a fitting number for a name that literally means earth-worker, because Threes make things grow, and they make growing look like joy.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone Opal, the flower Daisy and the colour Bright Green.
