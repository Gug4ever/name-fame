The Story of the Name

Theodore is Greek and says exactly what it means: theos, god, and dōron, gift. The same two words reversed make Dorothea, so the two names are mirror images of one another. Rome took it as Theodorus, the Church carried it to Canterbury in a Greek monk's luggage, Russia softened it to Fyodor, and America turned a president into a teddy bear. The gift has travelled well.

The Numerology of Theodore

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Theodore add up to the number 9 — “The Giver”. Nines close the number cycle, and they behave like people who know it: generous with what they have, because they have seen where hoarding ends. They take on causes larger than one life, a nation's freedom, a people's homeland, a continent's forests, and they work at them with a warmth that makes the size of the task look ordinary. A Nine gives, and calls it doing his job.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mars, the gemstone Aquamarine, the flower Aster and the colour Bright Green.
