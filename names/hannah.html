The Story of the Name

Hannah is the original. The Hebrew Channah comes from the root chanan, to show favour, and it belongs first to the woman who prayed at Shiloh without making a sound and was given Samuel. Greek scripture spelled it Anna, and Europe spun out Anne, Annette, Nancy and a hundred more — but the older spelling never left, and the English Bible handed it back. Its bearers keep the two halves of the vow: plain speech, and a refusal to be moved.

The Numerology of Hannah

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Hannah add up to the number 1 — “The Asking Voice”. Ones begin things, and a Hannah begins them by saying the thing out loud that everybody else has agreed not to mention. It is not noise: the first Hannah prayed so quietly the priest thought she was drunk, and still got exactly what she came for. A One sets her own terms, keeps them long after the fuss has died down, and is very hard to talk out of a promise once she has made it.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Sun, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Narcissus and the colour Midnight Blue.
