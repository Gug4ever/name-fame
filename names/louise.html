The Story of the Name

Louise is the French feminine of Louis, and behind both stands the Frankish Hlodwig, from hlod, fame, and wig, battle. Latin smoothed it into Ludovica, and Renaissance France handed it back to women as Louise. Its bearers have fought with other tools than swords: a regent's treaty, a nurse's rota, a barricade, a chisel, and a poem plain enough to memorise.

The Numerology of Louise

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Louise add up to the number 9 — “The Carer”. Nines close the number cycle, and they spend it looking outward. A Nine notices who has been left out of the arrangement and then does something structural about it: a nursing order, a union, a school, a sentence that says the hard thing plainly. She is not sentimental, and she is not easily embarrassed. She is practical, stubborn, and willing to be disliked for exactly as long as the work takes.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mars, the gemstone Citrine, the flower Lily of the Valley and the colour Slate.
