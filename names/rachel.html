Rachel

Rachel means: ewe — the gentlest of the flock.

The Story of the Name

Rachel is the Hebrew Rahel, a ewe: the shepherd's word for the gentlest animal in the flock, and one of the oldest names still given to daughters. Gentle is not the same as soft, and its bearers have proved it. A Rachel painted flowers for seventy years, another waited out a storm of insecticide advertising and won, and another cast the inside of a house in concrete and left it standing in the rain.

The Numerology of Rachel

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Rachel add up to the number 11 — “The Kindled Lamp”. Eleven is the master number of quiet light, and a Rachel carries it the way a lamp carries a flame: steadily, and for other people. Elevens are noticing people. They see the thing nobody has mentioned yet, sit with it until they are sure, and then say it plainly, which is usually the moment the room changes. The patience is not passivity. It is the long fuse on a considerable charge.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Moon, the gemstone Emerald, the flower Narcissus and the colour Meadow Green.
