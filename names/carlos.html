Carlos

The Story of the Name

Carlos is the Spanish and Portuguese shape of the Germanic karl, the free man, and it entered Iberia with a crown on: the first Carlos on a Spanish throne, in 1516, was also the emperor Charles V. Then the name did what Spanish and Portuguese did, and crossed the ocean. Today Brazil alone counts nearly a million and a half Carloses, and the free man answers to a guitar in San Francisco, a tango in Buenos Aires and a racket in Murcia.

The Numerology of Carlos

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Carlos add up to the number 5 — “The Traveller”. Fives are the travellers of the number chart, and their element is the crossing. A Five leaves Tijuana for San Francisco, Toulouse for Buenos Aires, Berlin for the River Plate, and arrives sounding as if he had always been there. Curious, quick and allergic to fences, he learns by moving, carries what he learns to the next place, and is understood in every language he lands in.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Marigold and the colour Sea Teal.
