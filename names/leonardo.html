Leonardo

Leonardo means: brave as a lion.

Number 3 — The Maker of Marvels Jupiter ✦ Ruby ✦ Chrysanthemum ✦ Royal Blue

The Story of the Name

Leonardo joins the Latin lion to the old Germanic word for brave, and the combination has been living up to itself for fifteen centuries. A hermit who freed prisoners carried it first; then it crossed the Alps, put on Italian, and belonged forever to the man from Vinci who painted the world's most famous smile while designing flying machines in mirror writing. Lion-hearted, and lion-curious too.

The Numerology of Leonardo

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Leonardo add up to the number 3 — “The Maker of Marvels”. Threes create the way lions breathe: constantly, confidently, and in every direction at once. A Three's desk holds the painting, the invention, the song and the theorem side by side, because to him they were never separate subjects, only different ways of asking how the world works. His gift is joyful expression, and his secret is that curiosity never feels like work.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone Ruby, the flower Chrysanthemum and the colour Royal Blue.
