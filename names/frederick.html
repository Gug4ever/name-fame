The Story of the Name

Frederick unites two old Germanic words, frid, meaning peace, and ric, meaning ruler or power, into one of history's most crowned names. Emperors and kings wore it across Germany, Denmark and Prussia, yet its greatest bearers ruled other kingdoms entirely: Frederick Douglass ruled the podium, Chopin the piano, Banting the laboratory, and Olmsted the green heart of a city. Peaceful power, kept in a name.

The Numerology of Frederick

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Frederick add up to the number 7 — “The Thoughtful Crown”. Sevens rule from the inside out: they master a subject completely, in private, and only then step forward. That is Frederick's way. He is the king who plays the flute, the escaped slave who read his way to freedom, the surgeon who lay awake over an idea about the pancreas. A Seven's authority is never borrowed; it is earned page by page, and worn as lightly as peace itself.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Larkspur and the colour Garden Green.
