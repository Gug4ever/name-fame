The Story of the Name

Florence grows from the Latin florens, flourishing, the word Romans used for anything in full blossom. They gave it to a city on the Arno, Florentia, the flourishing one, and the city repaid the compliment by flowering into the Renaissance. As a given name it has kept the promise ever since: Florences have flourished as nurses, sprinters, composers, geologists and singers, blooming exactly where they were planted, and often where nobody thought a flower could grow.

The Numerology of Florence

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Florence add up to the number 6 — “The Flourishing Heart”. Sixes are the gardeners of the human family: they tend, they heal, they make things grow, and they cannot walk past anything wilting without watering it. It suits a name that means flourishing, because a Six measures her own success by how everything around her is blooming. Give a Six a ward, an orchestra, a classroom or a stage, and she will hand it back to you in flower.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Venus, the gemstone Opal, the flower Narcissus and the colour Midnight Blue.
