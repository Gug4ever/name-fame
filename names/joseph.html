The Story of the Name

Joseph comes from the Hebrew Yosef, the hopeful prayer Rachel spoke over her newborn son: may God add, may God increase. It is a name that promises more is coming, and its bearers have kept the promise generously: Josephs have added antiseptic to surgery, symphonies to music, balloons to the sky, prizes to journalism, and a thousand retold myths to the world's common story.

The Numerology of Joseph

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Joseph add up to the number 1 — “The Generous Pioneer”. Ones go first, and a Joseph goes first in order to give: the name means God will add, and Number One is how the adding gets done. He is the brother who plans the granary before the famine, the surgeon who tries the new method while colleagues scoff, the man who steps into the balloon nobody has flown. A One's confidence is not for show; it is a tool he uses to open doors, and then he holds them open for everyone behind him.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Sun, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Daffodil and the colour Midnight Blue.
