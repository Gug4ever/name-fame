The Story of the Name

Charlotte is the feminine of Charles — from the Germanic 'karl', a free man: freedom, made hers. French courts polished it, an English queen made it beloved, a Yorkshire parsonage made it literary — and a small princess has made it newly royal for the century ahead.

The Numerology of Charlotte

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Charlotte add up to the number 3 — “The Storyteller”. Threes are the storytellers of the number chart, and expression is their element. They turn life into narrative: they write it, paint it, play it, and somehow make everyone around them feel like a main character too. They carry wit and warmth in equal measure, with a stubborn creative core underneath. A Three does not wait to be offered a stage; she builds one out of whatever the room has to offer.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone Lapis Lazuli, the flower Larkspur and the colour Deep Red.
