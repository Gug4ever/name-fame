The Story of the Name

Thomas comes from the Aramaic Ta'oma — 'twin': the name of the apostle who would not believe until he touched the truth with his own hands. That honest doubt became a compliment. For two thousand years Thomas has been the name of the ones who check, test, build and prove — inventors, philosophers, statesmen — believers of the verified.

The Numerology of Thomas

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Thomas add up to the number 22 — “The Master Builder”. Twenty-two is the rarest number in the chart: the master builder. It combines a vision big enough to redraw the world with the patience to lay every brick of it personally. Where other people either dream or do, a Twenty-Two does both, turning lightning into light bulbs, ideas into republics, and honest doubt into solid knowledge.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Uranus, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Larkspur and the colour Forest Green.
