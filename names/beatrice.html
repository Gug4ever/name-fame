Beatrice

Beatrice means: she who blesses — and, underneath, the traveller.

Number 9 — The Guide Mars ✦ Pearl ✦ Poppy ✦ Vesper Violet

The Story of the Name

Beatrice is a Latin name with a slipped foot. It began as Viatrix, the feminine of viator, a traveller or wayfarer, and early Christians, hearing beatus in it, wrote it Beatrix instead: the blessed one. Both meanings stayed. Dante sent his Beatrice travelling through heaven, and the name has been carried since by women who went a long way and blessed what they found when they got there.

The Numerology of Beatrice

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Beatrice add up to the number 9 — “The Guide”. Nines close the circle of the number chart: the givers, the finishers, the ones who can see the whole road. A Nine is at her best half a step ahead of somebody else, pointing out what they had not noticed and then declining the credit for it. She is generous past prudence, patient with beginners, and quietly impossible to steer off a course she has chosen.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mars, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Poppy and the colour Vesper Violet.
