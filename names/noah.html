Noah

The Story of the Name

Noah is the Hebrew Noach, from a root meaning to rest or settle, and Genesis adds a second sense the moment the name is given: this one will comfort us. It is a builder's name before it is a sailor's, and its bearers have kept the habit of patient construction: a dictionary twenty years in the making, a peace society, a museum built from junk in the desert, and a lap of the track timed to the hundredth.

The Numerology of Noah

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Noah add up to the number 2 — “The Steady Hand”. Twos are the partners and peacemakers of the number chart, and the Noahs show what that looks like when it is put to work. A Two builds for other people: the dictionary is for the reader, the peace society is for the next generation, the ark is for everyone who fits aboard. He prefers a job done properly to a job done loudly, keeps his temper in a storm, and is the one still there when the water goes down.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Moon, the gemstone Opal, the flower Narcissus and the colour Slate.
