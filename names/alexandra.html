The Story of the Name

Alexandra is the feminine of the Greek Alexandros — 'alexo', I ward off, joined to 'andros', of men. It is one of the oldest names in Europe still in daily use: a Mycenaean scribe wrote it on a clay tablet three thousand years ago, and Sparta kept a shrine to the Alexandra of that tablet long after.

The Numerology of Alexandra

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Alexandra add up to the number 8 — “The Quiet Sovereign”. Eights are handed real power and expected to carry it, and an Alexandra carries hers without fuss. This is the number of the woman who takes the seat nobody has offered a woman before, sits in it as though it had always been hers, and then does the work so steadily that the argument is over. She is patient with decades, impatient with a wasted afternoon, and she almost never raises her voice.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Saturn, the gemstone Black Onyx, the flower Daffodil and the colour Deep Scarlet.
