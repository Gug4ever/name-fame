The Story of the Name

Karen is Denmark's own form of Katharina, the Greek name Rome heard as katharos — 'pure'. Danish speakers wore the long Latin down to two plain syllables and kept them: Karen is in the old ballad books, on farmhouse doors, in the parish registers of every Danish island. It is the northern, working spelling of a very grand name — and it has carried women to Africa, to Aleppo and to the Abel Prize.

The Numerology of Karen

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Karen add up to the number 22 — “The Master Builder”. Twenty-two is the second master number, and it is the practical one. Where eleven sees the vision, twenty-two lays the foundations, hires the masons and stays until the roof is on. A Twenty-two is patient with detail and impatient with talk, and she measures a life by what is still standing in it fifty years later. The great Karens have built farms, libraries, villages, theories and whole fields of study.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Uranus, the gemstone Jade, the flower Chrysanthemum and the colour Lagoon Teal.
