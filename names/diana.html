The Story of the Name

Diana descends from the oldest Latin root for the divine and the daylight sky: dius, the shining. Rome gave the name to its goddess of the moon and the hunt, protector of women and wild things, and the goddess never quite let go. Its modern bearers have hunted bigger game: hit records, gorilla poachers, ocean crossings, and the hearts of entire nations.

The Numerology of Diana

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Diana add up to the number 2 — “The Queen of Hearts”. Twos are the number chart's diplomats and companions: they work by connection, not command. A Diana of this number reads a room the way her goddess read a forest, moving quietly, missing nothing, and standing instinctively between the vulnerable and whatever hunts them. She would rather hold a hand than hold court, which is precisely why courts never forget her.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Moon, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Rose and the colour Terracotta.
