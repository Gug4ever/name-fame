The Story of the Name

Rebecca is the Greek and Latin spelling of the Hebrew Rivqah, a name whose meaning scholars still argue about: the root seems to carry the sense of tying fast, so it has been read as a bond, a tether, even a snare. Its bearers have kept the binding and dropped the trap: a Salem farmwife whose neighbours tied their names to hers in writing, a Philadelphia woman who bound a scattered community together with schools, and a gymnast who held on through three ruined knees.

The Numerology of Rebecca

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Rebecca add up to the number 1 — “The Deciding Voice”. Ones are the number chart's decision-makers, and a One's gift is not loudness but timing: she listens to the whole argument, waits until everybody else has run out of road, and then says the sentence that settles it. Having said it, she does not take it back. That is why Ones end up at the centre of things they never set out to run, and why they are hard to talk out of anything.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Sun, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Daffodil and the colour Ink Navy.
