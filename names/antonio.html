The Story of the Name

Antonio is Antonius come south and stayed. One of Rome's oldest family names, glossed by tradition as priceless, beyond value, and sweetened by Greek folklore with anthos, the flower, it became the everyday name of Latin Europe. Italy, Spain and Portugal have handed it down for twenty centuries to violin makers and grammarians, to sailors, sculptors and songwriters, and it has never once sounded out of place.

The Numerology of Antonio

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Antonio add up to the number 7 — “The Patient Maker”. Sevens work at their own depth and their own speed, and the world catches up afterwards. They are the ones who will spend a winter on one violin, one page, one marble shoulder, because they can feel a difference nobody else can see yet. A Seven finishes things quietly, without announcements, and what he finishes tends to outlast everyone who doubted it.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Aquamarine, the flower Rose and the colour Royal Blue.
