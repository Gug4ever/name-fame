The Story of the Name

William comes from the Germanic Willahelm — 'will' joined to 'helmet': desire armoured in protection. Carried to England by a conqueror in 1066, it became the most royal of English names — borne by kings, poets, scientists and rebels for a thousand years.

The Numerology of William

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of William add up to the number 7 — “The Seeker”. Sevens are the thinkers and truth-hunters of the number chart. They are drawn to whatever lies beneath the surface, they trust knowledge over noise, and they have the patience to understand what other people rush past. A Seven leads not by shouting but by knowing, and when one finally speaks, the whole room leans in to listen.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Rose and the colour Rose Pink.
