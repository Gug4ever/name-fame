Luis

The Story of the Name

Luis is the Spanish shape of the old Frankish Chlodowig, hlod for fame and wig for battle, the name that Latin polished into Ludovicus and France into Louis. Spain shortened it to two syllables and sent it across the Atlantic, where it took root in every country from Mexico to Argentina. Its greatest bearers fought their battles with a poem, a cine-camera, a pink wall or a bubble chamber.

The Numerology of Luis

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Luis add up to the number 7 — “The Patient Eye”. Seven is the number of the observer, the one who looks longer than everyone else and sees what the rest missed: a thin layer of clay in a cliff, the way afternoon light falls on a plain wall, the gap a defender leaves for half a second. A Seven works alone without loneliness, keeps his own counsel, and when he finally speaks, the room finds he has been right all along.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Turquoise, the flower Rose and the colour Slate.
