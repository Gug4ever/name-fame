The Story of the Name

Patrick is the Latin patricius, the word Rome used for the families descended from its first fathers, the patres. It began as a rank and ended as a name, and it was carried out of Britain into Ireland by a boy who was taken there in chains. Ireland made it Pádraig and gave it back to the world, so that a Roman word for high birth is now worn by half the parishes of the island and by Patrices, Patricios and Patryks everywhere else.

The Numerology of Patrick

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Patrick add up to the number 33 — “The Master Teacher”. Thirty-three is the rarest master number in the chart: the teacher who refuses to keep what he knows. A Patrick of this number learns something the hard way, usually a good deal harder than he would have chosen, and then spends the rest of his life making sure the next person does not have to. The gift is patience with beginners. The work of a lifetime is remembering that his own turn matters too.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Venus, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Daffodil and the colour Ember Orange.
