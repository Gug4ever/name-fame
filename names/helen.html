Helen

Helen means: light · torch.

Number 8 — The Light-Keeper Saturn ✦ Golden Topaz ✦ Poppy ✦ Baltic Teal

The Story of the Name

Helen comes from the Greek helene, the torch, kin to the very word for the blaze of the sun. Myth gave it to the most beautiful woman in the world, whose face launched a thousand ships, but its true bearers have been light-bringers of a steadier kind: a deaf-blind girl who lit the way for millions, an empress who found relics by lamplight, and the first Briton to see the sun rise from space.

The Numerology of Helen

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Helen add up to the number 8 — “The Light-Keeper”. Eights are entrusted with real power, and a Helen of this number treats hers as a lighthouse keeper treats the lamp: something to be kept burning for other people's sake. She achieves at the highest level, in surgery, in space, in parliament, and then turns the achievement outward, illuminating paths she will never walk herself. The torch in her name is not decoration; it is a job description.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Saturn, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Poppy and the colour Baltic Teal.
