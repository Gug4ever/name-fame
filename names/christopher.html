The Story of the Name

Christopher comes from the Greek Christophoros, the bearer of Christ, born of the legend of a giant who carried a mysterious child across a raging river and found he had carried the weight of the world. Carrying has been the name's business ever since: Christophers have carried a cathedral onto London's skyline, carried travellers safely on medallions, carried a hovercraft across the Channel, and carried a guitar into orbit.

The Numerology of Christopher

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Christopher add up to the number 4 — “The Faithful Carrier”. Fours are the ones you trust with the heavy things. They build foundations that outlast fashions, keep their promises across rivers and decades, and carry what others cannot lift without ever making a show of the weight. It suits a name whose very first story is a giant wading through a flood with a child on his shoulders. A Four's greatness is load-bearing: quiet, tested, and still standing centuries later.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Uranus, the gemstone White Opal, the flower Larkspur and the colour Pale Blue.
