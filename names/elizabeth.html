The Story of the Name

Elizabeth comes from the Hebrew Elisheva — 'God is my oath': a promise worn as a name. It arrived with a mother in scripture, was carried by a medieval saint who filled her cloak with roses, and then crowned itself twice over in England. Few names have ruled longer, reformed more, or written better — Elizabeth is a dynasty of one word.

The Numerology of Elizabeth

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Elizabeth add up to the number 7 — “The Seeker”. Sevens are the deep readers of the world. Behind a composed surface they are always studying: people, books, and whole situations at once. They act only when they truly understand, which is why their moves so rarely miss. It is the number of the wise ruler and the patient scientist: knowledge gathered first, then the decisive stroke. A Seven's trust is earned slowly, and kept forever.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Chrysanthemum and the colour Ember Orange.
