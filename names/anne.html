The Story of the Name

Anne comes from the Hebrew Hannah — 'grace, favour': a gift asked for and granted. Tradition gave it to the grandmother of Jesus, queens gave it to France and England, and history's Annes have carried grace into the hardest places — a Tower cell, a hidden attic, a pirate deck, a dark well where a teacher spelled water into a child's hand.

The Numerology of Anne

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Anne add up to the number 7 — “The Seeker”. Sevens look deeper than anyone else in the number chart. Behind a composed surface runs a constant current of observation: they read rooms, read people, and read between every line on the page. It is the number of diarists and of teachers, of minds that must truly understand before they speak. When a Seven finally acts, it lands, because nothing she does is done unconsidered.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Jade, the flower Larkspur and the colour Warm Gold.
