Diane

Diane means: divine · of the shining sky.

Number 6 — The One Who Holds The Line Venus ✦ Diamond ✦ Rose ✦ Deep Crimson

The Story of the Name

Diane is the French form of Diana, the Roman goddess of the moon, the hunt and wild places, whose own name comes from the oldest Latin root for the bright daylight sky. France took the goddess for its own at the Renaissance and gave the spelling back to the world. Its bearers have hunted their own quarry: a five-minute mile, a mountain gorilla, a photograph nobody else would have taken.

The Numerology of Diane

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Diane add up to the number 6 — “The One Who Holds The Line”. Sixes are the number chart's guardians, and a Diane guards by staying put. Where others argue, she simply stands where she has decided to stand, and the argument has to come to her. It looks like patience and it is actually resolve: a Six has worked out what she is responsible for, and she does not put it down. Her devotion is practical, unshowy and extremely hard to dislodge.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Venus, the gemstone Diamond, the flower Rose and the colour Deep Crimson.
