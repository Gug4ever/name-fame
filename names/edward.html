The Story of the Name

Edward is pure Old English: ead, meaning wealth and blessing, joined to weard, the guardian. It is one of the very few Anglo-Saxon names the Norman Conquest could not dislodge, because a sainted king had already made it untouchable. Eight King Edwards followed, and so did guardians of subtler treasures: of health, of music, of light on a lonely diner, of nonsense itself.

The Numerology of Edward

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Edward add up to the number 1 — “The Guardian-King”. Ones lead, and this One leads the way a good king does: by being the person everything valuable can be trusted to. His very name means guardian of riches, and the number agrees. He goes first into the unknown, keeps what matters safe behind him, and hands the treasure on larger than he found it. Decisive, protective and steady: the whole kingdom sleeps better for him.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Sun, the gemstone Emerald, the flower Marigold and the colour Slate.
