Jane

Jane means: God is gracious.

The Story of the Name

Jane is the English daughter of Johanna, itself from the Hebrew Yochanan: God is gracious. The same root gives France its Jeanne and Ireland its Sinéad, which means Joan of Arc and Jane Austen are, by name at least, sisters. Plain Jane was always a slander: the name belongs to the sharpest pens, the deepest forests and the firmest consciences in this book.

The Numerology of Jane

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Jane add up to the number 3 — “The Voice”. Threes are the voices of the number chart, and Jane's is the clearest of them: expression honed until a single sentence can carry a whole world. A Three of this kind observes quietly, sometimes for years, in ballrooms or forests or city streets, and then says the thing everyone else almost noticed. After she speaks, nothing looks quite the same.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone Diamond, the flower Lily of the Valley and the colour Forest Green.
