The Story of the Name

Paul comes from the Latin Paulus, meaning small or humble, and history has enjoyed the joke ever since: few names have done bigger things. It was chosen by the apostle whose letters crossed the ancient world, and it has belonged to painters who rebuilt the way we see, to a Beatle, to popes and physicists and chefs. A small name, carried enormously far.

The Numerology of Paul

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Paul add up to the number 5 — “The Messenger”. Fives are the messengers of the number chart: freedom, movement and connection are their native elements. They learn by travelling, whether across oceans or across ideas, and they cannot keep a good discovery to themselves. A Five's letters get answered, his songs get sung along to, and his news, somehow, always arrives exactly where it needs to go.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Aquamarine, the flower Rose and the colour Imperial Gold.
