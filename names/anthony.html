The Story of the Name

Anthony descends from Antonius, one of Rome's oldest family names, which tradition glossed as priceless, beyond value, and Greek folklore sweetened with anthos, the flower. Both meanings stuck, and both came true: Anthonys have painted priceless portraits, composed four seasons of music, built a cathedral like a garden of stone, and written the world's most treasured little book about a prince.

The Numerology of Anthony

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Anthony add up to the number 7 — “The Priceless Mind”. Sevens go deeper than the market can follow, which suits a name that means priceless. They are the ones who look through the microscope longer, listen to the sonata again, and walk into deserts, literal or otherwise, to find what cannot be bought. A Seven's treasures are invisible until he shows them to you, and then you wonder how you ever missed them.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Citrine, the flower Rose and the colour Pale Blue.
