Miguel

Miguel means: who is like God? — Iberia's oldest question.

Number 4 — The Deep Root Uranus ✦ Garnet ✦ Aster ✦ Ember Orange

The Story of the Name

Miguel is the Spanish and Portuguese form of the Hebrew Mikha'el, a question rather than a statement: who is like God? Castilian softened the Latin Michael into Miguel, Catalonia into Miquel, the Basque country into Mikel, and the name sailed with the caravels: from Andalusia to the Azores, from Guanajuato to Buenos Aires, hardly a town of the Hispanic world lacks a San Miguel.

The Numerology of Miguel

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Miguel add up to the number 4 — “The Deep Root”. Fours are the roots of the number chart. They go down before they go up, they hold in poor ground, and they keep holding when the weather turns. A Four would rather finish one true thing than start ten promising ones, and he is stubborn in the exact place where stubbornness is a virtue: on the point he believes to be true. Slow to be moved, impossible to shift once he has taken hold.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Uranus, the gemstone Garnet, the flower Aster and the colour Ember Orange.
