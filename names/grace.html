The Story of the Name

Grace is one of the rare names that never needed translating: it is the Latin gratia, the freely given gift, worn as a name. The Greeks personified it three times over as the Charites, dancing hand in hand. Its bearers have kept the family resemblance: grace under fire in a lighthouse rowboat, grace on a movie screen and a palace balcony, and grace in the computer code that taught machines to speak English.

The Numerology of Grace

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Grace add up to the number 7 — “The Still Water”. Sevens are the still waters of the number chart, and stillness, as every Grace demonstrates, is not the same as standing still. Beneath a composed surface, a Seven is always working: computing, rehearsing, watching the storm and judging the moment to launch the boat. When she moves, it looks effortless, and only she knows exactly how much depth that took.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Jade, the flower Daisy and the colour Sunrise Amber.
