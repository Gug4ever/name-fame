The Story of the Name

Benjamin comes from the Hebrew Binyamin — 'son of the right hand': the favoured place, the trusted side. Jacob's youngest and best-beloved gave the name its meaning, and history filled it with founders, composers and quiet geniuses who became everyone's most trusted hand.

The Numerology of Benjamin

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Benjamin add up to the number 5 — “The Explorer”. Fives are the explorers of the number chart: curiosity with momentum. Freedom is their natural element, and they collect experiences, questions and passport stamps of every kind. They learn by touching, invent by playing, and turn every constraint they meet into a door. A Five's favourite sentence, in any language, begins with the words what if.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Emerald, the flower Daffodil and the colour Midnight Blue.
