Emily

The Story of the Name

Emily comes from the Latin Aemilia — the name of a great Roman family, from 'aemulus': striving, emulating, the rival who makes everyone better. It softened through French into Emily and became the name of poets, engineers and pioneers who quietly out-worked their whole eras.

The Numerology of Emily

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Emily add up to the number 1 — “The Pioneer”. Ones carry origin energy: they are the number of beginnings. The first line, the first crossing, the first refusal to wait for anyone's permission, all belong to them. Independent to the bone and quietly certain, a One does not need the room to agree with her before she starts; she only needs a pencil. Wherever she chooses to walk becomes a path for others.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Sun, the gemstone Turquoise, the flower Poppy and the colour Baltic Teal.
