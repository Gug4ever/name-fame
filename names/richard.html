Richard

The Story of the Name

Richard is Germanic through and through: ric, meaning ruler or power, joined to hard, meaning brave and strong. The Normans carried it into England in 1066, a crusading king made it a legend, and it has never been out of use since. Its bearers have honoured both halves of the promise: brave enough to run the first locomotive, and steady enough to found a festival, a faith or a science.

The Numerology of Richard

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Richard add up to the number 7 — “The Bold Enquirer”. Sevens are the investigators of the number chart, and this Seven has a brave ruler's name to match. He is the one who takes the machine apart to see how it works, opens the safe to prove it can be opened, and drops the rubber ring into iced water in front of a committee. He does not need the room to agree; he needs the answer, and he is prepared to look foolish for as long as it takes to find it.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Daisy and the colour Forest Green.
