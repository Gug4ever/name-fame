The Story of the Name

Stephen comes from the Greek stephanos, the wreath of leaves set on a winner's head at the games, and a crown has sat on the name ever since. The first Christian martyr carried it, a king of Hungary made it a nation's founding name, and a king of England wore it through a civil war. Its modern bearers have kept winning laurels of their own: in physics, in film, on the football pitch and in the boldest computer company on Earth.

The Numerology of Stephen

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Stephen add up to the number 33 — “The Master Teacher”. Thirty-three is the rarest master number in the chart: the teacher who does not keep what he knows. A Thirty-Three learns a hard thing thoroughly, then finds the words that let everyone else in, whether the subject is black holes, a coral reef, a computer or a country's freedom. He is happiest with an audience, not for the applause but for the moment the idea lands.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Venus, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Narcissus and the colour Bright Green.
