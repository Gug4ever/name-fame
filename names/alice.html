Alice

Alice means: of noble kind.

Number 3 — The Wonder-Maker Jupiter ✦ Jade ✦ Narcissus ✦ Forest Green

The Story of the Name

Alice is the French-polished form of the old Germanic Adalheidis, noble of kind: the name of empresses long before it fell down a rabbit hole. One golden afternoon on an Oxford river, a real Alice asked for a story, and the name became wonder itself. Its bearers have kept both halves of the bargain: noble in the laboratory and the courtroom, wondrous absolutely everywhere.

The Numerology of Alice

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Alice add up to the number 3 — “The Wonder-Maker”. Threes are the imagination of the number chart, and an Alice of this number does imagination's bravest thing: she follows the question all the way down the hole. Curiouser and curiouser is her working method, in chemistry as much as in stories, and she reports back from wherever she lands with wit, wonder and perfect nerve. The world she returns to is always a little larger than the one she left.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone Jade, the flower Narcissus and the colour Forest Green.
