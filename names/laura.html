Laura

Laura means: the laurel — the victor's crown.

Number 8 — The Long Haul Saturn ✦ Citrine ✦ Marigold ✦ Deep Indigo

The Story of the Name

Laura is the laurel tree worn as a name: Latin laurus, the evergreen whose leaves crowned poets and generals and gave us both the laureate and the baccalaureate. It came through Laurentius, the man of Laurentum, the town of laurels, and it travelled almost unchanged into every European language. Petrarch made it the most sung name in poetry; its bearers have tended to earn the crown rather than wear it.

The Numerology of Laura

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Laura add up to the number 8 — “The Long Haul”. Eights are built for distance. Where other numbers sprint, an Eight paces herself, keeps the books, learns the ground and arrives with everything intact. She is practical about ambition and unembarrassed by hard work, and she tends to be handed responsibility early because she so obviously will not drop it. Given time, an Eight builds something that outlasts her.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Saturn, the gemstone Citrine, the flower Marigold and the colour Deep Indigo.
