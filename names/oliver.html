Oliver

Oliver means: the olive tree — peace, with deep roots.

The Story of the Name

Oliver descends from the olive tree — Latin 'oliva', the branch of peace itself — crossed with the old Norse Óleifr, 'ancestor's heir'. It entered legend beside Roland as the wise friend of the bravest knight, and has meant loyal counsel and quiet strength ever since.

The Numerology of Oliver

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Oliver add up to the number 9 — “The Peacemaker”. Nines carry the widest heart in the number cycle. They complete things: arguments, journeys, and other people's sentences when kindness requires it. They fight only for what truly matters, and they forgive faster than anyone thinks is wise. A Nine is an olive branch with a spine, and the world has never needed anything more.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mars, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Larkspur and the colour Baltic Teal.
