The Story of the Name

Isabella is the southern sister of Elizabeth — from the Hebrew Elisheba, 'God is my oath', turned golden in medieval Spain and Italy. It crowned queens in Castile, France and England, patronised the Renaissance, financed the crossing of an ocean, and never lost the sound of a promise kept.

The Numerology of Isabella

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Isabella add up to the number 7 — “The Discerning Eye”. Sevens are the discerning minds of the number chart. They gather knowledge the way collectors gather treasures: taking their time, checking twice, and keeping only what proves true. An Isabella of this number is reserved until she is certain, and immovable once she is. Whatever a Seven chooses to keep, from a painting to a promise, tends to keep its value.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Violet and the colour Deep Crimson.
