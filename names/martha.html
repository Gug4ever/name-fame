The Story of the Name

Martha is Aramaic before it is anything else: Mârtâ, the feminine of mar, master, and so the lady, the mistress of the house. Greek took it as Martha in the Gospels and Latin kept the spelling unchanged. The meaning has held: the Marthas of history tend to be the ones running the place, whether the place is a kitchen, a laboratory, a salon chain or a dance company.

The Numerology of Martha

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Martha add up to the number 7 — “The Steady Hand”. Sevens look inward before they look busy, and a Martha is the proof that the two are the same thing. She wants to know how the thing actually works — the body, the chromosome, the chord, the ledger — and then she wants it done properly. She is the one still in the room when the applause has gone, checking the count.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Larkspur and the colour Meadow Green.
