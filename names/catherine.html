The Story of the Name

Catherine comes from the Greek Aikaterinē, heard by Rome as katharos — 'pure': and rarely has purity commanded so much power. It is the name of a scholar-saint who out-argued fifty philosophers, of empresses who ruled Russia and France, and of a spelling for every taste — Catherine, Katherine, Kathryn — each one carrying the same clear fire.

The Numerology of Catherine

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Catherine add up to the number 11 — “The Illuminator”. Eleven is a master number, and it carries the visionary's charge. It sees further than the room it happens to be standing in: patterns before they form, reforms before they are thinkable, trajectories before the rocket has even been built. An Eleven carries extra voltage through her whole life, and the great Catherines have always known exactly what to do with it: light the place up.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Moon, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Chrysanthemum and the colour Deep Red.
