Amelia

The Story of the Name

Amelia rises from the Gothic 'amal' — work, vigour, striving — the root that named the Amali, the royal house of the Goths. Softened through Amalia in Europe's courts and polished in Georgian England, it became the name of women who simply went: across oceans, skies and every 'no' in the way.

The Numerology of Amelia

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Amelia add up to the number 5 — “The Adventurer”. This five is the adventurer: horizon-hunger, managed with a checklist. She is freedom's engineer, the one who plans the fuel and checks the weather, and then flies anyway. Restless, practical and impossible to keep on the ground, she measures her life in firsts and in fields crossed, and she files fear under the same heading as weather: useful information.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Larkspur and the colour Imperial Violet.
