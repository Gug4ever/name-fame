The Story of the Name

Andrew is Greek before it is anything else: Andreas, built on andreios, manly and brave, from aner, a man. It was already a common Greek name in Galilee when a fisherman called Andrew left his nets, and the Church carried it out along every trade route it had. It reached Scotland as a flag, Russia as a naval ensign and Italy as Andrea, a boy's name still. Its bearers have kept the root: they stand up.

The Numerology of Andrew

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Andrew add up to the number 11 — “The First Mover”. Eleven is a master number, and it belongs to the person who goes first and then goes to fetch somebody else. An Andrew of this number sees the thing before the room does, and his instinct is not to keep it: he brings his brother, he prints the drawings, he builds the library, he says the sentence nobody else will say aloud. He is calm about it, and immovable.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Moon, the gemstone Emerald, the flower Chrysanthemum and the colour Harbour Green.
