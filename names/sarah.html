Sarah

Sarah means: princess, noblewoman.

Number 2 — The Gentle Sovereign Moon ✦ Pearl ✦ Lily of the Valley ✦ Deep Crimson

The Story of the Name

Sarah is Hebrew for princess, and it is the only name in the Bible that God himself changed for a woman: Sarai, my princess, became Sarah, princess of multitudes. The promotion proved prophetic. Sarahs have ruled the stage in two centuries, built a business empire from a washtub, written America's best-loved nursery rhyme, and sung jazz with a voice the world simply called divine.

The Numerology of Sarah

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Sarah add up to the number 2 — “The Gentle Sovereign”. Twos rule the way the moon rules the tide: without armies, without shouting, and completely. They read the room before the room knows its own mood, hold partnerships together with invisible thread, and win by patience what force could never take. A Two's crown is made of trust, which is why nobody can steal it, and why a name meaning princess so often lands on a Two.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Moon, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Lily of the Valley and the colour Deep Crimson.
