Olivia

Olivia means: the olive tree — peace, bearing fruit.

The Story of the Name

Olivia grew from the Latin 'oliva' — the olive, tree of peace and plenty — and was already a rare name in medieval England when Shakespeare gave it to the countess of Twelfth Night and made it famous for good. Four centuries later, it is one of the most crowned girl's names of the new millennium.

The Numerology of Olivia

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Olivia add up to the number 5 — “The Bright Spirit”. This five is the bright spirit: light on her feet and quick in her mind. She makes rooms warmer and questions sharper at the same time, sociable without ever being shallow, adventurous without being reckless. She learns everything by loving it first. Wherever a bright spirit has passed, you will notice that things grow.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Rose and the colour Baltic Teal.
