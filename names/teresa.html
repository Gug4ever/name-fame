Teresa

Teresa means: the harvester — or the woman from Therasia.

Number 5 — The Seeker Mercury ✦ Pearl ✦ Marigold ✦ Royal Blue

The Story of the Name

Teresa is an old name whose root is genuinely uncertain. Scholars offer the Greek theros, summer, and therizo, to harvest, and also the island of Therasia beside Santorini. What is not in doubt is its first bearer: Therasia, the Spanish wife of Saint Paulinus of Nola in the fourth century. For a thousand years it stayed an Iberian name, and then one Castilian nun sent it round the world.

The Numerology of Teresa

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Teresa add up to the number 5 — “The Seeker”. Fives are the movers of the number chart, and a Teresa moves for a reason. She is restless in the useful way: she goes and looks, she asks the question nobody has asked, and she comes back with something. Fences bore her, whether they are made of rules or of other people's expectations, and she has a gift for walking round them without ever raising her voice.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Marigold and the colour Royal Blue.
