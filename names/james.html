The Story of the Name

James comes from the Hebrew Ya'aqov — the name Jacob, 'holder of the heel'. It travelled through Greek as Iakobos and Latin as Iacomus before English made it James. It is the name of kings, apostles, pioneers and rebels — and for a century, America's most constant companion.

The Numerology of James

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of James add up to the number 3 — “The Creator”. Threes are the performers and storytellers of the number chart. Energy, imagination and the gift of expression come naturally to them: they light up rooms, turn ideas into words and words into action. Where other people see routine, a Three sees material for a story. They succeed through joy rather than force, and their enthusiasm is contagious.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone Emerald, the flower Larkspur and the colour Rose Pink.
