Anna

Anna means: grace.

Number 3 — The Gracious Spark Jupiter ✦ White Opal ✦ Larkspur ✦ Royal Gold

The Story of the Name

Anna descends from the Hebrew Channah, grace or favour, the name of Hannah who prayed for a child and gave the world the prophet Samuel. Greek scripture smoothed it into Anna, Rome carried it across Europe, and every language kept it almost unchanged, as if nothing about it needed fixing. Graceful by definition, Annas have danced the world's most famous swan, written its bravest poems, and photographed the very first book of pictures.

The Numerology of Anna

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Anna add up to the number 3 — “The Gracious Spark”. Threes are the communicators and creators of numerology, and Anna's Threes express with their whole being: a poem, a pirouette, a photograph, a founded holiday. Where others hoard their gifts, a Three gives them away in public, gracefully, and makes it look easy. The grace in the name is not just elegance; it is generosity performed out loud, the talent for turning feeling into something the rest of us can see.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone White Opal, the flower Larkspur and the colour Royal Gold.
