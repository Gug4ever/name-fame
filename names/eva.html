Eva

Eva means: life — the living one.

Number 1 — The First Light The Sun ✦ Emerald ✦ Lily of the Valley ✦ Slate

The Story of the Name

Eva is the oldest name in the book, and the plainest: the Hebrew Ḥawwāh, from the verb to live, given in Genesis to the mother of all the living. Greek made it Heua, Latin made it Eva, and English trimmed it to Eve, but most of Europe kept the two open vowels. Its bearers have lived up to it with some energy: a First Lady who gave a nation the vote, a sculptor who made art out of rope and rubber, and a designer still at work at a hundred.

The Numerology of Eva

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Eva add up to the number 1 — “The First Light”. Ones go first, and an Eva goes first the way morning does: not by pushing, but by being there before anyone else is up. Her name means life itself, and Number One is life at its most direct: the idea before the committee, the sketch before the factory, the first voice in the hall. She does not wait to be asked, and she does not need the room's permission to begin.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Sun, the gemstone Emerald, the flower Lily of the Valley and the colour Slate.
