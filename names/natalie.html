Natalie

Natalie means: born at Christmas — the birthday itself.

The Story of the Name

Natalie is a date turned into a name. The Latin natalis means 'of birth', and dies natalis Domini was the Lord's birthday: Christmas. Girls born in that week were called Natalia, and the word travelled with them — Nathalie through France, Natalya through Russia, Natália through Portugal. It is one of the few names that tells you when its first bearers arrived.

The Numerology of Natalie

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Natalie add up to the number 8 — “The Quiet Engine”. Eights are the number chart's engineers. They like a thing to work, and they will stay with it long after the excitement has gone, because finishing is the part that counts. An Eight takes on weight willingly: the fund, the family, the institution that would collapse without her. She rarely announces any of it, and the announcement was never the point.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Saturn, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Narcissus and the colour Warm Gold.
