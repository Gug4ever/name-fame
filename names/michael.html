Michael

The Story of the Name

Michael comes from the Hebrew Mikha'el — not a statement but a battle-cry of a question: 'Who is like God?' It is the name of the archangel who leads the armies of heaven, and it has armed kings, emperors, artists and athletes for three thousand years.

The Numerology of Michael

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Michael add up to the number 6 — “The Guardian”. Sixes are the guardians of the number chart. Protection, harmony and the instinct to stand between others and harm come to them as naturally as breathing. They are the defenders and healers, the ones people turn to when things break, and they make wherever they stand feel safer. Theirs is strength placed entirely in the service of care.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Venus, the gemstone Jade, the flower Aster and the colour Deep Crimson.
