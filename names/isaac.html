Isaac

Isaac means: he will laugh — the laughter itself.

Number 6 — The Long Patience Venus ✦ Pearl ✦ Carnation ✦ Slate

The Story of the Name

Isaac is the Hebrew Yitzhak, he will laugh, and Genesis explains the joke itself: Sarah, told in old age that she would bear a son, laughed out loud, and the boy was named for it. Very few names carry a laugh in their meaning. It went into Greek as Isaak and Latin as Isaac and from there into every European language, and its bearers have been physicists, hymn writers, painters and storytellers, a great many of whom changed what the rest of us could see.

The Numerology of Isaac

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Isaac add up to the number 6 — “The Long Patience”. Sixes are the tenders of the number chart: they look after things, they set them in order, and they are happiest when the whole arrangement holds together without them having to say so. A Six will work at one problem for twenty years and call it a pleasure. What looks like slowness is usually care, and what comes out at the end of it tends to be something other people can live inside.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Venus, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Carnation and the colour Slate.
