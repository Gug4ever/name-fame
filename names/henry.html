Henry

The Story of the Name

Henry comes from the Germanic Heimirich — 'heim', home, joined to 'rich', ruler. It crossed to England with the Normans as Henri and crowned eight English kings, an emperor-saint, and a line of makers and builders who ruled workshops instead of realms.

The Numerology of Henry

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Henry add up to the number 7 — “The Builder of Depths”. This is the craftsman's seven: mastery, method and the patience to build what lasts. A Henry of this number works knowledge into wood, steel and law until it holds. He measures twice and speaks once, and what he leaves behind is too solid to fall. Home is what he builds, everywhere he goes, for everyone who follows him there.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Blue Sapphire, the flower Larkspur and the colour Sapphire Blue.
