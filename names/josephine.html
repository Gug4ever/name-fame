The Story of the Name

Josephine is the feminine of Joseph, and English took it from the French Joséphine, which began life as a pet form of Josèphe. Behind it lies the Hebrew Yosef: may God add, may God increase. The name stayed quiet for centuries until 1796, when a young general began calling his wife Joséphine, a name she had never used, and Europe copied him within a decade. It has been adding ever since, in every spelling from Josefina to Giuseppina.

The Numerology of Josephine

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Josephine add up to the number 11 — “The Quiet Beacon”. Eleven is two turned up to its brightest setting: the peacemaker's instinct for other people, made into a light they can steer by. A Josephine rarely shouts. She notices what a room is feeling before anyone says it, she gathers up the overlooked, and then she stands somewhere visible and refuses to move, which is how a whole crowd ends up walking towards her. Elevens carry other people's hopes lightly and their own standards very hard indeed.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Moon, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Violet and the colour Paris Teal.
