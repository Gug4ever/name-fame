Matthew

Matthew means: gift of God.

Number 9 — The Reckoner Mars ✦ Jade ✦ Aster ✦ Royal Blue

The Story of the Name

Matthew is the Hebrew Mattityahu, gift of Yahweh, carried into Greek as Matthaios, into Latin as Matthaeus, and into English by a tax collector who left his booth and wrote the first book of the New Testament. Every spelling keeps the same promise: Matteo in Italy, Mathieu in France, Mateo in Spain, Matvei in Russia, and its close cousin Matthias, from the same Hebrew root, across the German lands. A gift, given and handed on.

The Numerology of Matthew

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Matthew add up to the number 9 — “The Reckoner”. Nines close the number cycle, and they behave like people who have done the sums: they know what a thing costs and they hand it over anyway. A Nine keeps the account honestly and then spends it on somebody else. He finishes the long jobs, the ones with no applause at the end, and his generosity is practical and unsentimental, which is why it lasts.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mars, the gemstone Jade, the flower Aster and the colour Royal Blue.
