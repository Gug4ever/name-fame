The Story of the Name

Louis descends from the old Frankish Chlodowig, built from hlod, fame, and wig, battle: the name of Clovis, the king who first united the Franks. Eighteen French kings polished it, one of them into pure sunlight, but its greatest bearers won gentler battles: against rabies and darkness, against silence for the blind, and for a trumpet's right to make the whole world glad.

The Numerology of Louis

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Louis add up to the number 22 — “The Master Builder”. Twenty-two is the rarest number in the chart: the master builder, who dreams at full scale and then delivers at full scale too. Palaces, alphabets, sciences, songbooks: a Twenty-Two takes on projects that frighten whole committees, and finishes them. He has the patience of a mason and the ambition of a king, and history keeps what he makes.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Uranus, the gemstone Ruby, the flower Poppy and the colour Printer's Teal.
