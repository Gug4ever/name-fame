Christine

Christine means: a Christian — one who belongs to Christ.

Number 6 — The One They Lean On Venus ✦ Citrine ✦ Larkspur ✦ Warm Gold

The Story of the Name

Christine is the French and German form of the Latin Christiana, the feminine of Christianus: a Christian, one of Christ's own. Behind it stands the Greek Christos, the anointed one, and behind that a jar of scented oil poured over a head. The name began as a declaration of loyalty, and its bearers have gone on declaring things: at Bolsena, at the French court, from a Stockholm throne and outside a Gestapo cell at Digne.

The Numerology of Christine

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Christine add up to the number 6 — “The One They Lean On”. Sixes are the load-bearing walls of the number chart. A Six notices what a room needs before the room does, and quietly supplies it: the cup of tea, the difficult letter written, the awkward silence filled. The mistake people make is to read that as softness. A Christine of this number will carry a family, a team or a whole institution for years, and then, when something matters enough, will not move an inch.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Venus, the gemstone Citrine, the flower Larkspur and the colour Warm Gold.
