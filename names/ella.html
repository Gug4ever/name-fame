Ella

Ella means: the other one — and, in Hebrew, a goddess.

Number 3 — The Voice Jupiter ✦ Jade ✦ Violet ✦ Rose Pink

The Story of the Name

Ella came to England with the Normans, who wore the old Germanic alja, meaning other or foreign, as a name of its own. The English then folded it into something easier: a short form of Eleanor and Ellen, and later of Isabella. Its bearers have been the other voice in the room, the one that carries: a jazz singer who out-scatted the horns, an organiser who let the students lead, and a Swiss girl who sailed alone at the Olympics.

The Numerology of Ella

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Ella add up to the number 3 — “The Voice”. Threes are the communicators of the number chart, and every Ella in this book found a way to be heard: on a bandstand, in a courtroom, from a governor's chair, on a mule track in Turkestan. A Three does not wait for permission to speak. She finds the note, the sentence or the road, and other people follow because it sounds like the truth.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone Jade, the flower Violet and the colour Rose Pink.
