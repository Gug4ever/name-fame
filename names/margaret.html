The Story of the Name

Margaret comes, through Latin and Greek, from margarites: the pearl, the one jewel that grows rather than glitters. Queens of Scotland, England, France and Italy wore the name like the gem, and its bearers have kept proving the point: from a pizza named for a queen to the code that landed on the Moon, Margarets produce, layer by layer, things of unmistakable value.

The Numerology of Margaret

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Margaret add up to the number 11 — “The Luminous One”. Eleven is a master number, and it glows the way its name-gem does: from within. A Margaret of this number carries extra voltage behind a composed surface, seeing further than the room she stands in and saying so, whatever the room thinks. Like the pearl, she is made by patience under pressure, and like the pearl, the result needs no polishing.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Moon, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Chrysanthemum and the colour Slate.
