Victoria

Victoria means: victory — carried, not claimed.

Number 7 — The Sovereign Mind Neptune ✦ Amethyst ✦ Narcissus ✦ Deep Crimson

The Story of the Name

Victoria is Latin for victory itself — the winged goddess Romans set above every triumph. The name waited centuries for its queen; when she came, she gave it to an age, an ocean of places, and a way of carrying success: steadily, with gloves on.

The Numerology of Victoria

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Victoria add up to the number 7 — “The Sovereign Mind”. This seven is the sovereign mind: composure, depth, and judgment that needs no applause. She rules an inner realm first, reading, weighing and deciding in private, and the outer world follows her lead. Other people win moments; a Seven wins whole eras, simply by refusing to be hurried. The crown is optional, because the sovereignty is built in.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Narcissus and the colour Deep Crimson.
