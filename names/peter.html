The Story of the Name

Peter comes from the Greek petros, meaning rock or stone, and it began as a nickname with a mission. A fisherman called Simon was told: you are Peter, and on this rock I will build. Tsars took the name and built cities, composers built symphonies on it, and painters built empires of colour. It has been load-bearing ever since.

The Numerology of Peter

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Peter add up to the number 1 — “The Foundation”. Ones are the number of beginnings, and this One is the foundation stone. He is the first thing set down, the point everything else is measured from, the person others build on without always remembering to say so. Independent, decisive and completely dependable, a One of this kind does not chase the crowd. He stands still, and the crowd assembles around him.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Sun, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Rose and the colour Deep Crimson.
