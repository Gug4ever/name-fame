Clara

Clara means: bright, clear, famous.

Number 8 — The Luminous Achiever Saturn ✦ Pearl ✦ Poppy ✦ Rose Pink

The Story of the Name

Clara comes straight from the Latin clarus, the word Romans used for anything luminous: a clear sky, a ringing voice, a famous deed. The name entered history when a young noblewoman of Assisi took it as her banner, and it has kept its promise ever since. Claras have lit up concert halls, battlefields, laboratories and silver screens, each one making the world a little clearer, a little brighter, exactly as the name foretold.

The Numerology of Clara

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Clara add up to the number 8 — “The Luminous Achiever”. Eights build things that last, and a Clara builds them in full daylight. This is the number of quiet power used well: the nurse who organises the battlefield, the pianist who runs the family and the concert tour, the designer whose lamps still light rooms a century later. An Eight does not wait for permission or applause; she does the work so clearly and so completely that the world simply rearranges itself around what she has made.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Saturn, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Poppy and the colour Rose Pink.
