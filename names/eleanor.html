Eleanor

Eleanor means: the shining light.

Number 7 — The Shining Seeker Neptune ✦ Garnet ✦ Rose ✦ Midnight Blue

The Story of the Name

Eleanor arrived in history as Alienor, borne in the Occitan south by the heiress of Aquitaine who carried it onto two thrones, and tradition read it as alia Aenor, the other Aenor, then as the shining one, kin to the Greek Helen, the torch. The gloss proved prophetic: Eleanors have lit the way as queens who ruled half of Europe, scientists who studied what others ignored, and a First Lady who helped write the rights of all mankind.

The Numerology of Eleanor

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Eleanor add up to the number 7 — “The Shining Seeker”. Sevens carry their own lantern, which suits a name that means the shining light. They are the ones who read the documents nobody else opens, study the insects nobody else respects, and keep asking the question after the room has moved on. An Eleanor with a Seven's soul does not wait to be handed the truth; she rides out, notebook in hand, and finds it, then holds it up so everyone can see.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Garnet, the flower Rose and the colour Midnight Blue.
