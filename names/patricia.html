Patricia

The Story of the Name

Patricia is the feminine of the Latin patricius, the word Rome used for the families descended from its first fathers, the patres. It began as a rank and ended as a compliment: high-born, and expected to behave like it. The name travelled with the Church as Patrizia, reached Britain through Scotland, and in the middle of the twentieth century became one of the commonest names in the English-speaking world.

The Numerology of Patricia

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Patricia add up to the number 5 — “The Quicksilver”. Fives are the quick ones: curious, adaptable, bored by a closed door and very hard to hold in one place. A Patricia of this number learns by going and finds out by asking, and where somebody else sees a rule she sees a question. The gift is range. The work of a lifetime is choosing, out of everything she could do, the few things she will finish.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Pearl, the flower Poppy and the colour Royal Blue.
