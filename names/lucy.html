Lucy

Lucy means: light — the one born at daybreak.

Number 7 — The Lamp Carrier Neptune ✦ Golden Topaz ✦ Narcissus ✦ Rose Pink

The Story of the Name

Lucy is the Latin lux, light, worn as a name. The Romans gave Lucius and Lucia to children born at first light, and the early Church gave the name a saint, Lucia of Syracuse, whose feast falls in the darkest week of the year and is still kept with candles. Its bearers have kept faith with the meaning: a lamp in the Resistance, a lens at the Bauhaus, and a girl on Prince Edward Island who wanted a story with nonsense in it.

The Numerology of Lucy

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Lucy add up to the number 7 — “The Lamp Carrier”. Sevens are the number chart's quiet observers, and a Lucy shows what the quiet is for. She looks longer than other people before she moves, and by the time she moves she has usually seen the whole route: the ridge above the cloud, the lorry's timetable, the chapter nobody else thought a girl could publish. A Seven does not need the room's attention. She needs the facts, and a little light to read them by.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Neptune, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Narcissus and the colour Rose Pink.
