The Story of the Name

Daniel comes from the Hebrew Daniyyel — 'God is my judge': a name that answers to no earthly power. It belongs first to the prophet who read dreams in Babylon and walked out of the lions' den — and since then to pioneers, thinkers and quiet heroes who kept their nerve in every den history offered.

The Numerology of Daniel

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Daniel add up to the number 9 — “The Humanitarian”. Nines are the humanitarians of the number chart: compassion with a spine. They feel what everyone in the room is feeling, and then they act on it. As the completers of the number cycle they are wise beyond their errand, generous past all common sense, and drawn to causes far bigger than themselves. A Nine walks into the lions' den simply because someone in there needs company.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mars, the gemstone Diamond, the flower Larkspur and the colour Royal Blue.
