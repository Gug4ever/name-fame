Sophia

The Story of the Name

Sophia is the Greek word for wisdom itself — the sister of philosophy ('love of sophia') and the name Byzantium gave its greatest church. Carried by empresses, mathematicians and a girl who faced tyranny with a leaflet, it is one of the few names that is also humanity's highest aim.

The Numerology of Sophia

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Sophia add up to the number 5 — “The Seeker of Wisdom”. This five is the seeker of wisdom: curiosity that refuses every fence. She hunts understanding the way other people hunt treasure, through books, across borders, and straight past the rules that said not for you. Quick-minded, adaptable and quietly daring, she knows that the deepest wisdom is portable: it travels in questions, and she always packs plenty.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Aster and the colour Wisdom Violet.
