Alexander

The Story of the Name

Alexander comes from the Greek Alexandros — 'alexein', to defend, joined to 'andros', of men. It was worn by Homeric princes and Macedonian kings before one young Macedonian king carried it to the edge of the known world — and made it the most conquering name in history.

The Numerology of Alexander

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Alexander add up to the number 3 — “The Torchbearer”. Threes are the torchbearers of the number chart: vision, energy and the rare gift of carrying other people along. They turn ideas into movements; they speak and people follow, they imagine and things actually get built. A Three is restless with the possible and generous with the flame, never guarding the fire when he can pass it on.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Jupiter, the gemstone Blue Sapphire, the flower Poppy and the colour Deep Crimson.
