The Story of the Name

Liam is Ireland's own cut of William. The Normans carried Willelm across the Irish Sea in the twelfth century, the Irish made it Uilliam, and in time the everyday short form Liam stood on its own. For two centuries it was barely heard outside Ireland; today it is the most popular boys' name in the United States.

The Numerology of Liam

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Liam add up to the number 8 — “The Builder”. Eights are the builders of the number chart: the ones who turn will into something you can stand on. They are ambitious without being showy, they finish what they start, and they carry responsibility as if it weighed nothing. An Eight measures himself by what is left standing after he has gone, and he tends to leave a great deal.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Saturn, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Rose and the colour Atlantic Teal.
