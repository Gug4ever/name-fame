The Story of the Name

Angela is the Greek angelos, a messenger, borrowed by the Church for its winged couriers and then handed straight back to ordinary daughters. The name kept the job description rather than the wings: its bearers carry word from one place to another — an abolitionist's letter into the burning South, a physicist's calm into a chancellery, a teacher's lessons onto spools of paper fifty years before anyone said e-reader.

The Numerology of Angela

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Angela add up to the number 22 — “The Master Builder”. Twenty-two is the rarest number in the chart: the master builder, who dreams at full scale and then delivers at full scale too. A Twenty-Two takes on the thing everyone agrees is too big — a school system, a prison argument, a country — and works at it steadily until it stands up. She is patient, unhurried and almost impossible to divert once the plan is drawn.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Uranus, the gemstone Amethyst, the flower Carnation and the colour Rose Pink.
