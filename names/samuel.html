The Story of the Name

Samuel comes from the Hebrew Shemu'el, meaning God has heard: it was the name a long-hoping mother gave the son she had prayed for. Her boy grew up to hear a voice in the night and answer, here I am, and the name has belonged to great listeners ever since. Dictionary makers, diarists, code inventors and explorers: Samuels pay attention, and then they act.

The Numerology of Samuel

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Samuel add up to the number 8 — “The Steward”. Eights are the stewards of the number chart: the ones trusted with real things, because real things stay safe in their hands. They combine ambition with conscience, building institutions, fortunes and reputations that are meant to be handed on rather than hoarded. An Eight hears what is actually needed, provides it, and keeps honest accounts the whole way.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Saturn, the gemstone Ruby, the flower Poppy and the colour Navy Blue.
