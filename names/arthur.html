Arthur

Arthur means: bear · noble one.

Number 5 — The Quester Mercury ✦ Aquamarine ✦ Poppy ✦ Warm Gold

The Story of the Name

Arthur most likely rises from the old Celtic artos, the bear: the strongest creature in the northern woods, and a king's name waiting to happen. One legendary war-leader made it immortal, and a round table grew around him. Ever since, Arthurs have been questers: for lost cities and lost chords, for justice, for the summit of Everest and the source of a perfect pint.

The Numerology of Arthur

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Arthur add up to the number 5 — “The Quester”. Fives are the questers of the number chart: born for the open road and the unfinished map. They need a horizon the way other numbers need a plan, and they are at their best when something worthy is missing and must be found. A Five of Arthur's kind gathers companions as he goes, because he knows the oldest rule of quests: nobody completes one alone.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Mercury, the gemstone Aquamarine, the flower Poppy and the colour Warm Gold.
