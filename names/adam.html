The Story of the Name

Adam is the oldest name in the book, and one of the plainest: in Hebrew it simply means a human being. Genesis makes a pun of it, forming the first man from the adamah, the red ground, so that the creature carries the name of the stuff he is made of. The name travelled without translating — Adam in Poland and Bohemia, Adamo in Italy, Adán in Spain, Adem in Turkey — and it has never once gone out of use in three thousand years.

The Numerology of Adam

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Adam add up to the number 1 — “The First Light”. Ones begin things. That is the whole of it, and it is rarer than it sounds: most people would much rather join something already moving than stand at the front of an empty field and put the first spade in. An Adam is the one who starts, names what he finds, and takes the consequences of having gone first, which include being blamed for the weather.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet The Sun, the gemstone White Pearl, the flower Narcissus and the colour Deep Navy.
