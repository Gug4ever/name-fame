Eric

Eric means: ever-ruler — the one who rules alone.

Number 8 — The Long Haul Saturn ✦ Citrine ✦ Lily of the Valley ✦ Deep Indigo

The Story of the Name

Eric is the Old Norse Eiríkr, built from ei, ever or always, and ríkr, ruler, a word that is cousin to the Latin rex and the German Reich. It came ashore in England with Viking settlers, was carried through the Middle Ages by a line of Scandinavian kings, and was revived for English-speaking parents by the Victorians. The Erics who followed have kept the sense of a man who goes his own way: west across an empty ocean, out of a Sunday heat, into the quiet at the end of a piano piece.

The Numerology of Eric

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Eric add up to the number 8 — “The Long Haul”. Eights are the long-haul people of the number chart: ambitious, patient and unbothered by how far off the finish is. They take on the things that need years rather than afternoons, keep their nerve when the work stops being interesting, and measure themselves by what is still standing afterwards. An Eric of this number would rather be trusted than admired.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Saturn, the gemstone Citrine, the flower Lily of the Valley and the colour Deep Indigo.
