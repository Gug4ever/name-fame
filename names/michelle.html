The Story of the Name

Michelle is the French daughter of Michael, the Hebrew Mikha'el: not a statement but a challenge, 'Who is like God?', the war-cry of the archangel who leads heaven's armies. For centuries the feminine form stayed rare, a Micaela here, a Michelina there. Then a Dieppe schoolgirl renamed herself Michèle Morgan, the Beatles sang the name in French, and for two years running it was the second most popular girl's name in America.

The Numerology of Michelle

In the Pythagorean tradition, the letters of Michelle add up to the number 4 — “The Groundbreaker”. Fours are the builders of the number chart, and a Michelle builds where nobody has built before. She lays her foundations on ground other people called impossible: a rally podium, a presidential sash, a racecourse, an Oscar stage. Her method is not dash but preparation, one gallop, one rehearsal, one case file at a time, so that when the door finally opens she is already standing there, ready.

Its four emblems in classical tradition: the planet Uranus, the gemstone Golden Topaz, the flower Poppy and the colour Royal Blue.
